Building B · Thursday 12 November, 10:30

Fire drill: what to do

The alarm sounds for about 30 seconds. Leave by the nearest exit and gather at assembly point 3 on the car park.

When the alarm sounds

  1. Stop what you are doing. Leave your bag and coffee.
  2. Close windows and doors behind you on the way out.
  3. Take the stairs, never the lift. Keep to the right.
  4. Report to your floor warden at assembly point 3.
  5. Stay until the warden says the building is clear.
